1. Risk Assessment Methodology

This framework employs a qualitative risk assessment methodology aligned with ISO 31000:2018 and COSO ERM principles. Risks are evaluated across five categories: Operational, Cyber, Compliance, Financial, and Strategic.

The inherent risk is assessed before controls are applied. After documenting existing controls, the residual risk is calculated. Both likelihood and impact are rated on a 1–5 scale to produce a risk score (Likelihood × Impact), which determines the rating tier.

Assessment Approach:

  • Workshops with business unit leaders and subject matter experts
  • Review of incident reports, audit findings, and external threat intelligence
  • Cross-functional risk committee review and sign-off
  • Alignment with regulatory requirements (GDPR, SOX, PCI-DSS, ISO 27001)

2. Risk Appetite Statement

The organisation maintains a low to moderate risk appetite across all enterprise risk domains. The Board of Directors sets the overall tolerance, with specific limits delegated to the Executive Leadership Team and risk owners.

🛑 Zero Tolerance
Regulatory violations, data breaches, fraud, safety incidents
⚠️ Low Appetite
Reputational, cyber, financial loss, strategic misalignment
📊 Moderate
Operational disruptions, minor compliance gaps, IT failures
✅ Higher Appetite
Innovation risk, strategic experimentation, market initiatives

8. Glossary of Terms

Inherent Risk — risk before any controls
Residual Risk — risk after controls are applied
Risk Appetite — amount of risk willing to accept
Risk Tolerance — acceptable variation around objectives
Control — measure that modifies risk
KRI — Key Risk Indicator; early warning metric
KPI — Key Performance Indicator for control effectiveness
Threat — potential cause of an unwanted event
Vulnerability — weakness that can be exploited
Likelihood — probability of a risk event occurring
